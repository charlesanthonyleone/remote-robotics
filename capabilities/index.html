Approach

Human in the loop

Every Remote Robotics system is designed around a trained operator. Robots handle low-level stability, footing and collision avoidance; people choose objectives and approve task actions. This keeps accountability with humans and keeps operators at a safe distance from hazards.

Our systems are not weapons and are not designed to carry them. We nonetheless align our design approach with the principles of DoD Directive 3000.09, “Autonomy in Weapon Systems” (updated January 25, 2023), which calls for “appropriate levels of human judgment over the use of force.” We cite it as a design reference, not as a claim of compliance, review or endorsement.
