About

Company

Remote Robotics is a new, early-stage research and development firm focused on human-supervised robotic systems for hazardous and remote environments.

Mission

Extend human reach, not human risk.

We develop robotic platforms and a common teleoperation interface that let trained people work in places that are dangerous, remote or unreachable. Our work is at concept stage: we are defining requirements, building analyses and early models, and seeking partners and research funding.

Past performance: none yet. Remote Robotics is a new entrant with no contracts or customers to date.

Development path

Three steps

  1. 01

    Concept and analysis

    Requirements, mission analysis and design studies. TRL 1–2. Current stage.

  2. 02

    Component prototypes

    Bench and lab validation of key subsystems and the DEEPLINK interface. Target TRL 3.

  3. 03

    Integrated demonstrators

    Partner-supported field demonstrations in relevant environments.

Heritage

An old idea, new tools

Legged machines have a long history. In 1893 Lewis A. Rygg of St. Paul, Minnesota was granted U.S. Patent 491,927 for a “Mechanical Horse”, a pedal-driven mechanism that walked on four legs. DESTRIER revisits the idea with modern actuation and control.
