Company overview

Early-stage R&D firm developing legged, subsea and aerial robotic systems operated by trained people through a common teleoperation interface. All systems are at concept stage (TRL 1–3).

Core competencies

  • Legged mobility concepts for extreme terrain (ARACHNE, DESTRIER)
  • Immersive teleoperation and shared control (DEEPLINK)
  • Coordinated aerial construction concepts (HIVEFORGE)
  • Human-in-the-loop system design

Mission areas

Expeditionary logistics · Cold-region / Arctic · Hazardous-terrain reconnaissance · Subsea and seabed · Space telerobotics · Expeditionary construction

Differentiators

  • One operator interface across ground, subsea and aerial systems
  • Operator-approved task actions by design
  • Unarmed systems; inspection, logistics and construction roles
