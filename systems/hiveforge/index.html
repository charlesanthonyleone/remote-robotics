HIVEFORGE

Coordinated aerial construction for expeditionary sites

Concept render. In development.

Summary

Mission

HIVEFORGE is a concept for coordinated drones that place blocks and deposit material to build simple structures, such as berms and barriers, at remote or expeditionary sites without large crews on the ground.

Concept of operations

A small team defines a structure on a site plan. HIVEFORGE drones are tasked in sequence, while a supervisor monitors progress and approves each build stage. The concept targets force-protection berms and basic site preparation.

Capabilities

Key capabilities

  • Multi-drone task coordination under human supervision
  • Block placement and material deposition concepts
  • Stage-by-stage build approval by a supervisor
  • Reduced crew exposure at remote sites
