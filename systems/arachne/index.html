Systems / Arachne

ARACHNE

Multi-legged climbing robot for hazardous and vertical terrain

Concept render. In development.

Summary

Mission

ARACHNE is a concept for an eight-legged robot that climbs and traverses terrain wheeled and tracked platforms cannot: rock faces, rubble, crevasse walls and steep slopes. It is planned in several sizes around a common control architecture.

Concept of operations

An operator team deploys ARACHNE from a safe standoff. The robot moves to a point of interest under supervised autonomy, holding footing on its own, while the operator directs inspection and sampling tasks over a remote link. Every task action requires operator approval.

Capabilities

Key capabilities

  • Eight-leg gait concept for vertical and broken terrain
  • Standoff sensing and inspection in hazardous areas
  • Modular payload bay for sensors or sampling tools
  • Scalable family (small, medium, heavy) sharing one control stack

Design targets

Headline targets

Configuration
8 legs, 3 size classes
Control
Supervised autonomy, operator-approved tasks
Target terrain
Slopes, rubble, ice and rock faces
