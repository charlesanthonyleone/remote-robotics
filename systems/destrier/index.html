DESTRIER

Quadruped logistics carrier for dismounted teams

Concept render. In development.

Summary

Mission

DESTRIER is a concept for a quadruped cargo carrier that moves loads with a dismounted team over terrain where vehicles cannot follow. It draws on a long lineage of legged-mechanism ideas, including the 1893 Rygg “Mechanical Horse” patent.

Concept of operations

DESTRIER follows a team or travels between waypoints with supplies, batteries and equipment, reducing the load each person carries. Operators can take direct control at any time through a handheld or DEEPLINK station.

Capabilities

Key capabilities

  • Legged mobility for rough, unimproved terrain
  • Follow-me and waypoint travel under operator supervision
  • Open cargo deck with standard tie-down points
  • Remote takeover through DEEPLINK
