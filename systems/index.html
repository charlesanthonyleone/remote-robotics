Concept systems

Four systems sharing one principle: the machine goes into danger, a trained human stays in control. All are concept-stage designs; capabilities shown are design targets.

Concept render

ARACHNE

Multi-legged climbing robot for hazardous and vertical terrain

ARACHNE is a concept for an eight-legged robot that climbs and traverses terrain wheeled and tracked platforms cannot: rock faces, rubble, crevasse walls and steep slopes. It is planned in several sizes around a common control architecture.

Stage: Concept, TRL 1–2

Concept render

DESTRIER

Quadruped logistics carrier for dismounted teams

DESTRIER is a concept for a quadruped cargo carrier that moves loads with a dismounted team over terrain where vehicles cannot follow. It draws on a long lineage of legged-mechanism ideas, including the 1893 Rygg “Mechanical Horse” patent.

Stage: Concept, TRL 1–2

Concept render

DEEPLINK

Immersive teleoperation station for remote and subsea systems

DEEPLINK is a concept for a teleoperation station that gives a trained operator an intuitive, high-fidelity view and control of a remote robot. It is designed as the common human interface for all Remote Robotics systems, including subsea and space telerobotics.

Stage: Concept, TRL 1–2

View Deeplink →
Concept render

HIVEFORGE

Coordinated aerial construction for expeditionary sites

HIVEFORGE is a concept for coordinated drones that place blocks and deposit material to build simple structures, such as berms and barriers, at remote or expeditionary sites without large crews on the ground.

Stage: Concept, TRL 1
