Research & development · Human-supervised robotics

Robots for places people should not have to go.

Remote Robotics is an early-stage R&D firm developing legged, subsea and aerial robotic systems operated by trained people from a safe distance.

Mission

Keep people in control, and out of harm’s way.

We design robots that extend human reach into hazardous, remote and extreme environments. Our systems pair robust mobility with a common teleoperation interface, so a trained operator makes the decisions while the machine takes the risk. We are a new entrant at concept stage (TRL 1–3) and are seeking research partners and funding to mature these designs.
